## Excerpts

### Cost formula revised 2026-10-28 (DESIGN_v9_direction.md, Matt, after the FL probe M-01; supersedes item 3)
```
leg cost_EC = ceil( U * vol * (B + J * jumps) * T[maintype] * V[tier] * H[DC] / 1000^3 / 1000 )   (B_bb, J_bb on a backbone leg)
```
- No cargo-class multiplier. `vol` = volume per unit (`get volume of ware`, seen on FL in the M-01 probe).
- `T[maintype]` per-mille from `get maintype of ware`: tech 1500; laser, shield, missile 2000; every other maintype (energy, natural, bio, food, minerals, ...) 1000.
- `V[tier]` per-mille by `get average price of ware`: under 1,000 -> 1000; under 10,000 -> 1250; under 100,000 -> 1500; higher -> 2000.
- `H[DC]` per-mille by the PAYING DC type, from its subtype like lib.LI.FDN.Dc.Range (never SS_DOCK_P_* constants): Player HQ (subtype 62) 800; Trading Station 900 (subtype not yet seen, so it falls into 'other' until a probe shows it); Equipment Dock (subtype 6) 1000; any other dock 1200.
- B 20, J 10 (station leg); B_bb 10, J_bb 2 (backbone leg); all milli-EC per volume unit. Literals at the top of the lib, not settings (M-02).
- Worked checks, 3 jumps from a Player HQ (FL probe data): 1,000 Energy Cells (vol 1) = 40 EC; 1,000 Ore (vol 8) = 320 EC; 1,000 Mosquito Missiles (missile x2.0) = 80 EC; 10 x 1 GJ Shield (vol 77, shield x2.0, tier x2.0) = 124 EC.

### Payment rules (DESIGN 'Decisions 2026-10-28', M-02 sections 2-3)
- Every transfer to or from a DC costs EC when [33] = 1. [33] 0 or null = free (cost 0, nothing taken).
- Who pays: the sending DC (DC to station, DC to DC). Station or dock to DC: the receiving DC pays (assumption, still to confirm with Matt).
- Take from virtual storage first (DC record `FDN.Ware.<Energy Cells>`[0]), then physical cargo.
- The DC never pays below its EC protect level = the larger of (a) the EC maintain level (record [2] when it is an INT > 0, else record [1] when INT > 0, else 0) and (b) the reserve floor = ceil(capacity x pct / 100), capacity = `$dc -> get max amount of ware SS_WARE_ENERGY that can be stored in cargo bay`, pct = `FDN.DC.Cfg`[1] when an INT 0-100, else [34] when an INT 0-100, else 25.
- Short: the other DCs in [30] (via lib.LI.FDN.Dc.List, excluding the payer) are asked in list order; each can give only what it holds above its own protect level. The transfer is paid only when payer + peers together cover the WHOLE cost (check first, then take): never partial.
- Network short: write a warning to the debug log (lib.LI.FDN.Log) AND directly to log 9910 (`write to log file 9910 append=[TRUE]`), add the cost to [3] 'deferred' of the DC FDN.Cost local, return FALSE. Line: `Cost: deferred need <c> EC, <DC> has <e>, network <n>`.
- `FDN.Cost` local (M-02 section 3) on the DC and on the served station: array of 4 built with `array alloc: size=4`: [0] EC total charged, [1] transfer count, [2] start time (playing time of the first charge), [3] deferred EC total. A peer that helped adds what it gave to its own [0].
