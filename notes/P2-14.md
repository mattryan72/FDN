P2-14: decision logging in the supply scripts

Closes
- D2 follow-up: every supply decision now leaves one debug line (only when the debug setting [7] is on)

What changed
- Supply.Dock: every ware in a dock pass writes exactly one line. A skip line names one reason: ST ware, no record, maintain not set, stock >= maintain (with or without surplus off), DC invalid, DC record missing, DC remote off (<= 0) or DC stock <= remote level. A ware that was supplied writes a 'moved=' line with maintain, stock, need, the DC remote level and the DC stock. A dock that is the DC writes 'skip: dock is DC'. The pass-end line is now 'Pass finished: wares=<n> moved=<n> skipped=<n>'.
- Supply.Factory: the shortfall line now reads '<factory> short <n> of <ware> at DC; buy allowed=<yes/no/no record>' (the old 'Buy N of ware ...' line was written before the can-buy check). The 'has', 'Remove' and 'requires' lines are only written for amounts above 0.
- Supply.Start: writes 'DC: <dc>' once per Scan run.
- Supply.Dock.Check: writes 'Dock pass start: docks=<n>'.
- Buy.Ware and Sell.Ware: a start line, a 'found' line for the partner, a 'Trade bought/sold 0' line, and Buy.Ware also logs 'amount is 0'. The 'No seller/buyer found' lines say how many cached sectors were searched.

Behaviour kept
- No behaviour change: no new waits, no new START, no new setting, no record is created or written for a log line. The new counters in Supply.Dock are plain locals that only the pass-end line reads.

Kept on purpose
- The pass-end counts are on Supply.Dock's own line, not on Dock.Check, because each dock runs as its own task.
- The 'DC changed' line waits for plugin.LI.FDN.DC.Change (P3-04b).
- The existing 'Clean from Dock Array' wording stays; price checks, drone failures and a gone partner are already logged by lib.LI.FDN.Trade.
- Supply.Dock keeps its existing 'Surplus' and 'Supply' lines; the new per-ware line adds the context.
